Preserving Javanese Culture and Honocoroko Script in the Era of Global Digital Transformation

"ꦲꦤꦕꦫꦏ꧈ ꦢꦠꦱꦮꦭ꧈ ꦥꦝꦗꦪꦚꦚ꧈ ꦩꦒꦧꦛꦔꦤ꧈ as a noble literary heritage that marks the civilization of the nation."

In the modern era marked by the currents of globalization and rapid technology, the existence of local culture and regional languages faces major challenges. PAWARTA is here as a real form of dedication to combine local wisdom with modern digital progress.

The Importance of Preserving Javanese Script

Javanese script, or better known as Honocoroko, is not just a traditional writing tool, but also contains deep philosophical values of life regarding harmony between humans, nature, and the Creator. With the existence of digital platforms like Pawarta.github.io, today's young generation has easier access to learn, read, and develop Javanese script through gadgets, computers, and artificial intelligence (AI)-based applications.

The Role of AI Technology and Social Media in News Journalism

Journalism in the AI era requires speed, accuracy, and wide reach. Pawarta utilizes cutting-edge technology including analytics integration like Google Analytics (G-GN5PGKKM3T) and AdSense optimization so that the information broadcasted is not only engaging but also complies with global standards. Through various interactive features such as voice translators, text-to-speech (TTS), and QR code readers, readers can experience a new experience when following the latest news.

Pawarta's Vision and Mission

  • Provide accurate, objective, and trusted news and information.
  • Preserve Honocoroko script and Javanese literature on digital platforms.
  • Develop an intelligent digital community responsive to AI technology.

By working together and supporting local platforms, we all have a moral responsibility to ensure that Javanese culture does not vanish with time, but instead blossoms and gains recognition on the international stage.
